Who We Are

Engineering at the Cutting Edge

EDGEMARK Engineering Consulting Ltd. is a structural engineering consultancy firm that bridges deep mathematical mechanics with bespoke software automation.

Founded and directed by Lawal Wasiu Kayode, EDGEMARK has contributed to pioneering engineering undertakings across Nigeria — notably the core-wall and lateral stability analysis of the 49-storey (200m tall) supertall residential tower at Eko Atlantic City, along with mid-rises in Victoria Island, 16-storey towers on Bourdillon Road, steel portal frames, and industrial infrastructure.

Beyond conventional finite element methods, we build proprietary computational software tools interfacing with ETABS, SAFE, and Tekla — automating rebar detailing, load imports, wind shear profiling, and data pipelines for organizations across the AEC sector.

Core Philosophy

Design Principles & Engineering Mindset

Balancing classical mechanics with forward-thinking performance design — safe, economical, and code-transcending.

"My design approach is primarily grounded in conventional structural principles. However, when the situation calls for it, I am not afraid to think beyond standard boundaries of codes, by employing performance-based design strategies to address unique challenges. Throughout, I maintain a strong focus on safety, economy, and sustainability, ensuring that every solution aligns with sound engineering judgment and contextual needs."

— Lawal Wasiu Kayode, Principal Structural Engineer
01

Safety First & Robust Redundancy

Ensuring primary load paths, ductile failure mechanisms, progressive collapse resistance, and strict serviceability limit states across all gravity and lateral loading scenarios.

02

Performance-Based & Demand Design

For supertall highrises and non-standard geometries, we look beyond prescriptive rules by performing staged construction, wind tunnel integration, P-Delta dynamics, and nonlinear demand-to-capacity verification.

03

Economy & Value Engineering

Smart material optimization: utilizing Post-Tensioned (PT) slabs for large spans, reducing self-weight, optimizing pile group configurations, and pruning reinforcement without compromising structural integrity.

04

Computational Automation

Eliminating human transcription error by scripting direct COM API pipelines into ETABS, SAFE, and detailing platforms — turning hours of manual modeling into minutes of precision execution.

Projected Tallest Residential in Africa

Structural Design of a 49-Storey (200m Tall) Residential High-Rise featuring Two Basement Parking Levels, a Suspended Swimming Pool, and Complex Organic Façade Geometry.

The concrete core-wall frame system was modeled and analyzed for gravity and lateral (wind) loads using CSI ETABS, going further to conduct a rigorous wind tunnel analysis to obtain precise aerodynamic coefficients and compare against numerical software results.

Critical Engineering Focus & Solutions:

Lateral Wind Governing Stability

Wind governs global overturning and deep foundation sizing; verified with wind tunnel aerodynamic data.

Suspended Swimming Pool

Hydrostatic load modeling, vibration control, and liquid retaining wall rebar under dynamic water sloshing.

Post-Tensioned (PT) Slabs

Lightweight floor system enabling architectural flexibility, crack control, and long column-free spans.

Punching Shear Demand/Capacity

Thorough D/C checks and internal shear reinforcement detailing around critical column connections.

Ramp Slope Stability & Load Transfer

Basement parking ramp geometry analyzed for torsional action and robust thrust transfer into core frames.

Long-Term Slab Deflection

Nonlinear creep, shrinkage, and cracked section stiffness checked against strict aesthetic tolerances.
