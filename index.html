Who We Are

Engineering at the Cutting Edge

EDGEMARK Engineering Consulting Ltd. is a Lagos based structural engineering firm with deep expertise in the analysis and design of complex, tall, and long span structures. We combine first principles structural mechanics with advanced computational tools to deliver solutions that are safe, efficient, and constructible.

Our team brings together experience across highrise residential towers, industrial facilities, deep foundation systems, and post tensioned slab design, underpinned by a commitment to code compliant, performance verified engineering at every stage.

We also develop bespoke software tools that automate repetitive engineering workflows: from ETABS model manipulation and rebar scheduling to wind response post processing, helping engineering teams deliver faster and with greater confidence.

High-Rise Structural Design

Post-Tensioned Slab Systems
